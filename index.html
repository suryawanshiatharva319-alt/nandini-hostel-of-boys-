ABOUT NANDINI HOSTEL

Affordable & Friendly Student Living

Nandini Boys Hostel provides affordable shared accommodation for students.

Rooms are available in 2, 3 and 4 sharing options. The bathrooms are separate from the bedrooms.

👤 Want the Complete Room for Yourself?

Single occupancy is possible. If one person wants to take the entire room, they must pay the fee for all the remaining people/beds in that room.

Example:

2-sharing → ₹4,000/month
3-sharing → ₹6,000/month
4-sharing → ₹8,000/month
